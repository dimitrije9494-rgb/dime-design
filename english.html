01 / ABOUT ME

Behind Dime Design is me — Dimitrije.

Design that combines creativity and functionality.

My background in International Marketing connected my interest in creativity, communication and the digital world with web design. Because of this, I don't look at websites through aesthetics alone, but also through the question of how a website will represent a brand, attract the right people and make a professional first impression.

Through Dime Design, I want to combine design and marketing to help small businesses, creatives and personal brands present themselves more effectively online.

To me, a good website isn't just about looking good — it should have a purpose, be easy to use and clearly communicate what a brand stands for.
